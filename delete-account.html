Delete your Kukula account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kukula and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm, then enter your password (or choose your Google account).

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email kukula.support@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my Kukula account”. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, profile photo link).
  • If you own a farm: the farm profile and all its records — flocks, batches, eggs, sales, feed, health, medicine, income and expenses, livestock trading, buyers and suppliers — and the invitations of your team members, who lose access.
  • If you are a team member of someone else's farm: only your membership. The farm and its records belong to the farm owner and are not deleted.
  • The copy of your records on your phone.

What is not deleted

  • Premium subscription: deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google keeps its own purchase records.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days, then removed automatically.
  • Google Drive backups you made are in your own Drive. Remove them in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Kukula → Delete hidden app data.
  • Backup files you exported and saved yourself.
