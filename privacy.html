Kukula Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Kukula is a farm record-keeping app for poultry farmers. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: kukula.support@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, also your Google profile photo link.
  • Farm profile: farm name, farm type, location (as you type it, e.g. a town) and app language.
  • Farm records you enter: flocks, batches, egg collection, sales, feed, health and vaccinations, medicine stock, income and expenses, and livestock trading.
  • Buyers and suppliers: names, phone numbers and notes you enter about the people you trade with, and amounts they owe or have paid.
  • Team: email addresses, names and roles of people you invite to your farm.
  • Premium purchases: the plan, purchase ID, Google Play purchase token and expiry date. Card and payment details are handled by Google Play; we never see them.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes, technical details (device model, Android version, app version and the error) are sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts list, photos, or files on your phone. The app has no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To store your farm records in your account so they are safe and available on any phone you sign in on.
  • To share your farm's records with the team members you invite, according to the role you give them.
  • To unlock Premium features you paid for.
  • To show reminders (vaccinations, low stock, payments due). Reminders are created on your phone.
  • To find and fix crashes.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics) stores your account and records on Google's servers.
  • Google Play processes Premium payments.
  • Google Drive (optional): if you turn on Drive backup, a backup file is saved in a private app folder of your own Google Drive. The app can only access that folder.
  • A copy of your records is also kept on your phone so the app works without internet. It is removed when you sign out.

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access rules allow only you and the team members you invite to read your farm's records.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all farm records at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. See how to delete your account, including without the app. Deleted records are removed from our database immediately. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days. Google keeps its own records of Play purchases.

Your choices

  • View and correct your records and profile in the app.
  • Export a copy of your records with Settings → Backup & Restore.
  • Farm owners can remove team members at any time; an invited person can decline the invitation.
  • Turn reminders off in Settings.
  • Delete your account and data as described above.

Children

Kukula is meant for farmers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
